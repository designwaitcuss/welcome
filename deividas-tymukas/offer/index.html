I design brands, and I build the AI tools my team uses

I started freelancing in 2012 and designed brands, packaging and campaigns for clients in Lithuania, the UK and Denmark. In-house I designed for Huawei, FL Technics and JETMS, where I made the brand book.

Today I'm the graphic and brand designer for the ZeroSum group. I run rebrands across the group's brands, design investor decks and quarterly fund reports, and build the campaign visuals for Lithuania, Latvia and Estonia.

I also build the AI tools my team works with. They make us faster, and the quality stays where it was.

Every brand I see now makes things with AI. It's fast, and it all looks the same. Same gradients, same emoji, the same words on every page.

Questions

What is slop?

Work that looks like AI made it. Default gradients, emoji as icons, the same words on every site. The kits check for it before you see a file.

Do I need to know Claude?

No. Each skill is one line you type. If your team isn't on Claude yet, take the done for you setup and I set it up with you.

Do I need After Effects?

Only for the video kit. The web, image and brand book kits run in Claude alone.

What if we don't have a brand book?

The brand book kit makes one from your logo, fonts and website. Every other kit reads it.

Do you see our files?

No. The kits run in your own Claude with your own files. I never see them.
